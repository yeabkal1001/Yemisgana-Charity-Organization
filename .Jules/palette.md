## 2026-06-25 - [Accessible Social Icons] \n **Learning:** Social media icons lacking ARIA labels are a critical accessibility issue. Adding dynamic 'aria-label' mappings over icons ensures semantic clarity. Added 'focus-visible' styles aligning with the project's 'focus-visible:ring-lime' and 'focus-visible:ring-offset-[var(--color-bg-primary)]' variables for consistent keyboard focus styling. \n **Action:** When implementing icon-only buttons or link items mapped from arrays, ensure an accessible label is provided along with 'focus-visible' states.
