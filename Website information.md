## **About Us** 

## **Our Mission** 

At Yemisgana Charity Organization, our mission is to ensure that every child in the Gurage Zone of Ethiopia has access to quality education in a safe and conducive learning environment. We are dedicated to the construction, renovation, and sustainable development of schools, aiming to provide students with the infrastructure they need to thrive academically and personally. 

## **Who We Are** 

Yemisgana Charity Organization was founded with a vision to tackle the pressing issue of inadequate educational ecosystem in the Gurage Zone, an area that faces significant challenges in terms of infrastructure and access to quality schooling. Through our work, we strive to create safe, modern, and well-equipped schools that inspire learning and development. 

Education is the key to breaking the cycle of poverty. By collaborating closely with local communities, government authorities, and other stakeholders, we focus on building and 

renovating schools to ensure that every child in the Gurage Zone has access to a safe, welcoming, and supportive learning environment 

## **What We Do** 

We focus on two main areas of impact: 

1. **School Construction** : We build new schools in underserved communities where access to educational facilities is limited. Our projects are designed with the needs of students and teachers in mind, ensuring that each school is a hub of knowledge and growth. 

2. **School Renovation** : Many schools in the Gurage Zone are operating in dilapidated buildings, lacking basic amenities and safety features. We renovate and upgrade existing schools to create modern learning environments that promote student well-being, including clean water, electricity, and proper sanitation facilities. 

Our work also involves providing educational resources, such as libraries, computers, and other materials, to further support the academic development of students. 

## **Why We Focus on the Gurage Zone** 

The Gurage Zone, located in the Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region of Ethiopia, is home to a vibrant community with a rich cultural heritage. However, the region has faced significant educational challenges, including overcrowded classrooms, inadequate school facilities, and a lack of resources for both students and teachers. 

By focusing our efforts on this area, we aim to empower local communities, break barriers to education, and help pave the way for future generations to succeed. We believe that access to quality education is the foundation for a brighter future, and that’s why we’re committed to making a lasting impact in the Gurage Zone. 

## **Our Approach** 

We work closely with local communities to understand their unique needs and ensure that our projects are designed with cultural sensitivity and sustainability in mind. Our approach includes: 

- **Community Engagement** : We believe in the power of community involvement. We work with local leaders, parents, teachers, and students to ensure that our projects are aligned with the community’s vision. 

- **Sustainability:** Our construction methods focus on long-term durability and practicality, ensuring that the schools we build and renovate continue to serve the needs of students and communities for years to come. 

- **Partnerships** : We collaborate with local governments, NGOs, and other organizations to amplify the impact of our work and build strong networks of support. 

## **Values** 

At Yemisgana Charity Organization, we are driven by the following core values: 

- **Integrity** : We uphold the highest standards of honesty and transparency in all our actions, ensuring that our work benefits the communities we serve. 

- **Collaboration** : We believe in the power of working together with local communities, government bodies, and other organizations to achieve meaningful change. 

- **Empowerment** : We aim to empower communities through education by providing them with the tools and resources they need to succeed. 

- **Sustainability** : We focus on creating lasting and practical solutions that continue to serve the needs of students and future generations. 

- **Equity** : We are committed to ensuring equal access to education for all children, regardless of their background or circumstances. 

## **Goals** 

Our goals are centered around creating long-term positive impacts for students: 

- **Expand Access to Education** : Build and renovate schools in underserved communities, ensuring that more children have access to quality education. 

- **Improve Learning Environments** : Create safe, modern, and conducive learning spaces that allow students to thrive academically. 

- **Increase Community Involvement** : Engage local communities in the construction and renovation process to foster ownership and ensure the sustainability of the projects. 

- **Enhance Educational Resources** : Provide schools with essential resources such as books, computers, vocational training programs, and other learning materials to support student success and equip them with practical skills for the future. 

- **Promote Gender Equality** : Ensure that both girls and boys have equal opportunities to learn, grow, and succeed in their education. 

## **Our Impact** 

Since we started our work, we have successfully built and renovated over 12 schools across the Gurage Zone, impacting the lives of over 7,000 students. Our work has not only improved the physical environment of schools but has also fostered a culture of learning and community development. 

But our journey doesn’t stop here. We are committed to expanding our reach and continuing to provide safe, functional, and inspiring learning spaces for children across the region.
